Overview

Join the Multimodal Systems & Vision Group at Microsoft Research India. In this role, you will collaborate with research scientists and research fellows on next-generation efficient Vision Transformers (ViTs) and Small Language Models (SLMs) customized for resource-constrained edge deployments. You will design, implement, and benchmark novel attention architectures, selective token-pruning methodologies, and post-training quantization schemes.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD degree program in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Electrical Engineering, or related quantitative field (Graduation timeline: Winter 2026 or Summer 2027).
  • Demonstrated proficiency in Python and deep learning frameworks (PyTorch or JAX), alongside firm grounding in linear algebra, multivariable calculus, and numerical optimization.
  • Prior coursework or research exposure to computer vision (object detection, segmentations, or generative models) and foundation model architectures.
  • Preferred: Hands-on experience with hardware-aware optimization, TensorRT, ONNX runtime, or low-bit quantization algorithms (AWQ, GPTQ).
