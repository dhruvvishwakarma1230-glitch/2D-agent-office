science About the Role & Research Scope

Join the Multimodal Systems & Vision Group at Microsoft Research India. In this role, you will collaborate with research scientists and research fellows on next-generation efficient Vision Transformers (ViTs) and Small Language Models (SLMs) customized for resource-constrained edge deployments. You will design, implement, and benchmark novel attention architectures, selective token-pruning methodologies, and post-training quantization schemes.
