infoOverview & Research Scope

Microsoft Research India's Multimodal Systems & Vision Group is seeking pre-final year student researchers for the Summer 2026 cohort. Interns work alongside world-class principal researchers on foundational vision transformers, small language models (SLMs), and efficient inference on resource-constrained hardware.

Selected interns contribute directly to high-impact preprints, participate in peer review cycles for tier-1 AI conferences (CVPR, ECCV, NeurIPS), and evaluate model architectures across multilingual and multimodal benchmarks.
